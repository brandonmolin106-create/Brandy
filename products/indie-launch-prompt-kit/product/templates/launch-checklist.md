# Launch checklist (42 checks, in order)

## Before the page goes live
1. Brief filled (templates/00)  2. Name searchable and not taken on the platform  3. Headline under 12 words  4. Trailer or GIF under 30 s, hook in first 2 s  5. 5+ screenshots, captioned  6. Thumbnail readable at 100 px wide  7. Price set per worksheet  8. Launch discount configured  9. FAQ written  10. Licence / refund text present  11. Download file attached and test-downloaded  12. Page proofread aloud

## 7 days out
13. Press kit page live  14. 20 press/creator contacts listed with a reason each  15. Email A sent  16. Teaser 1 posted  17. Devlog posted in home community  18. Email list told the date

## 3 days out
19. Teaser 2 and 3 posted  20. Keys sent to responders  21. Discord drops done  22. Support inbox checked and working  23. "Tomorrow" post drafted and scheduled

## Launch day
24. Store live, discount visible on the page (check incognito)  25. Launch thread posted and pinned  26. 3 subreddit posts, 2 hours apart, each tailored  27. Launch email sent  28. Email B to press  29. Every comment answered within the hour  30. First bug reports triaged  31. Numbers screenshot saved at end of day

## Days 2–7
32. Daily reply pass  33. Quote-post the best reactions  34. Player clips shared  35. Top bug fixed and update notes posted  36. Review ask to buyers (day 3)  37. Last-call post and email (day 5)  38. Discount off, price raised if 3+ ratings (day 7)  39. Retro written: what moved numbers, what didn't  40. Next update scoped from feedback  41. Thank-you to everyone who covered it  42. Calendar for the first content update set
