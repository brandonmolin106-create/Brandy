# Store page template (proven section order)

**Headline (under 12 words):** the hook as a promise, not a feature.
**Sub-headline (one sentence):** who it's for and what changes for them.

**Hero media:** trailer or 6-second GIF of the single most satisfying moment.

**Three proof points (one line each, icon optional):**
1. [number or concrete fact]
2. [number or concrete fact]
3. [number or concrete fact]

**Body (120–180 words):** problem → what this does instead → what it feels like to use → what's inside. No adjectives you can't prove.

**What you get (bullets, 5–8):** nouns first. "40 levels", not "lots of content".

**Social proof:** one quote, or "used by N people" only if true. Leave empty rather than fake it.

**FAQ (5 questions):** price, refunds, platform, updates, licence.

**Closing line + button text:** button says the action and the price: "Get the full game · A$5".
