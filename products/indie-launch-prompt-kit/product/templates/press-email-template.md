# Press and creator emails

## Email A — announcement (send 7 days before launch)
Subject options: "{{PRODUCT}}: [hook in 6 words], out [date]" · "[Audience] game about [hook], launching [date], key inside"

Hi [first name],

[One sentence on why them specifically: a piece they wrote, a game they covered.]

{{PRODUCT}} is {{ONE_LINE}}. It launches [date] on {{PLATFORMS}} at {{PRICE}}.

The bit people react to: {{HOOK}}.

Press kit (trailer, screenshots, GIFs, fact sheet): [link]
Key / review copy: [key or link]

No pressure to cover it. If you'd like a different key or a build with debug cheats, reply and it's yours.

[Name], [studio], [X handle]

## Email B — launch day (send morning of launch)
Subject: "{{PRODUCT}} is out today, key inside"

Hi [first name],

{{PRODUCT}} is live: [store link]. Launch price {{PRICE}} for [n] days.

30-second trailer: [link]. Press kit: [link]. Key: [key].

Thanks for reading,
[Name]
