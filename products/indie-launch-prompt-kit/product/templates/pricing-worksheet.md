# Pricing worksheet

**1. Comparable prices.** List 5 products your buyer already pays for in this category: [name, price, what's included]. Your price sits inside that band unless you can say in one sentence why not.

**2. Ladder.**
- Free tier / demo: what's in it, what it stops at, where the upgrade button lives.
- Launch price: __ (band low end for a new store with no ratings)
- Full price: __ (set now, raise after 10 sales and 3 ratings)
- Bundle: __ (two of your products at 70% of combined)

**3. Discount maths.** Launch discount __% for __ days or first __ buyers. Net per sale after platform fee (Gumroad 10% + 30c, itch your share, Steam 30%): __.

**4. Break-even.** Hours spent × what your hour is worth = __. Divide by net per sale = __ sales to break even. If that's over 200 for a new store, cut scope or raise the price.

**5. Regional.** Steam: use the recommended regional table. Gumroad: one currency, consider a purchasing-power code for 3 regions.
