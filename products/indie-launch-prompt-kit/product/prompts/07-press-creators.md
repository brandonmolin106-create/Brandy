# 07 · Press and creators (10 prompts)

### 7.1 Press list builder
```
Act as a PR researcher. Describe the 5 kinds of outlets and creators most likely to cover {{PRODUCT}} for {{AUDIENCE}} (e.g. niche newsletters, YouTube reviewers at 5k–50k subs, podcast hosts). For each kind: how to find 4 of them (exact search phrases), what they need from me, and the one-line reason I'd give for why them.
```

### 7.2 Press email (personalised)
```
Act as a PR writer who gets replies. Using templates/press-email-template.md, write the announcement email for [name] at [outlet], who recently [covered/wrote X]. Under 150 words, subject line under 50 chars, the hook in sentence two, key and press kit links, no pressure close. Voice {{VOICE}}.
```

### 7.3 Creator pitch
```
Act as a creator-relations manager. Write a DM-length pitch (under 90 words) to a YouTuber or streamer who covers {{AUDIENCE}}'s interests: why their audience, what the clip-worthy moment is ({{HOOK}}), the key, and an offer to make a custom build or asset if useful.
```

### 7.4 Press kit page
```
Act as a web copywriter. Write the press kit page for {{PRODUCT}}: fact sheet (name, developer, release date, platforms, price, links), a 100-word description, a 40-word description, 5 key features, the hook in one sentence, asset list with download labels, contact, and a short bio.
```

### 7.5 Follow-up sequence
```
Act as a PR writer. Write a 3-touch follow-up sequence after the press email: day 4 (new asset or detail, under 60 words), launch day (it's out, key reminder, under 50 words), day 10 (numbers or a player story, under 70 words). Each must give something new, never just "checking in".
```

### 7.6 Key distribution plan
```
Act as a launch manager. Plan key or review-copy distribution for {{PRODUCT}}: how many keys, who gets them (press, creators, communities, giveaways), how to track who received what, and the message template for each group. Include a note on avoiding key resellers.
```

### 7.7 Interview answers
```
Act as a media trainer. Write my answers to the 10 questions press and podcasters ask indie creators (origin, hardest part, what's different, who it's for, price reasoning, what's next, tools, advice, a mistake, where to get it). Each under 80 words, voice {{VOICE}}, quotable first sentence.
```

### 7.8 Award and festival list
```
Act as a festival scout. List 8 awards, showcases, jams or festivals where {{PRODUCT}} ({{TYPE}}) could be submitted, with what they look for, typical deadlines by month, fee if any, and a one-line fit note. Flag any I should skip and why.
```

### 7.9 Collab offer
```
Act as a partnerships lead. Write 3 collaboration offers to other creators who serve {{AUDIENCE}}: a bundle, a cross-promo post swap, and a guest piece. Each under 120 words with the specific benefit to them stated before mine.
```

### 7.10 Coverage thank-you
```
Act as a gracious founder. Write 3 thank-you messages for coverage: a public quote-post (under 200 chars), a private DM (under 80 words), and an email (under 100 words) that also offers an exclusive for next time.
```
