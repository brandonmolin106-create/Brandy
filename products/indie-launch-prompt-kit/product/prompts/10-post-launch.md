# 10 · Post-launch (10 prompts)

### 10.1 Retro
```
Act as a product coach. Run a launch retro for {{PRODUCT}} from these facts: [paste numbers, what was posted where, what happened]. Output: what moved the numbers (ranked), what cost time and moved nothing, three things to repeat, three to drop, and the single experiment for the next launch.
```

### 10.2 Update roadmap from feedback
```
Act as a product manager. I'll paste user feedback and reviews. Cluster them into themes with counts, rank by impact × ease, and propose the first content or feature update for {{PRODUCT}} with a one-paragraph scope and a release-notes headline.
```

### 10.3 Update announcement
```
Act as a release writer. Write the announcement for {{PRODUCT}} v[1.1]: X post under 240 chars, email (subject + 90 words), and store-page "What's new" block. Lead with the change users asked for most.
```

### 10.4 Sale timing
```
Act as a marketplace analyst. For {{PRODUCT}} on {{PLATFORMS}}, list the sale events over the next 6 months worth joining, the discount depth that fits a product at {{PRICE}}, how often to go on sale without training buyers to wait, and the copy for the sale badge.
```

### 10.5 Evergreen content
```
Act as a content strategist. List 10 evergreen pieces (posts, videos, pages) that would keep sending {{AUDIENCE}} to {{PRODUCT}} for a year: title, format, the search phrase it targets, and the one line that links to the product without being an ad.
```

### 10.6 Testimonial harvest
```
Act as a marketing ops person. Write the message asking happy buyers for a short testimonial (under 60 words), the 3 questions to ask so the answers are usable, and the format to display quotes on the store page with permission wording.
```

### 10.7 Cross-sell
```
Act as a merchandiser. For buyers of {{PRODUCT}}, write the post-purchase message (shown in the download area or receipt) that thanks them, gives one tip, and mentions one related product of mine with a code. Under 90 words.
```

### 10.8 Vol. 2 decision
```
Act as a product strategist. Given {{PRODUCT}}'s results: [paste], decide between a Vol. 2, a major free update, a bundle, or moving on. Lay out the expected effort, revenue range and risk for each, and recommend one in two sentences.
```

### 10.9 Refund reply
```
Act as a support lead. Write the refund reply for {{PRODUCT}}: grant it without argument, ask one optional question about why, mention the free version if there is one, and leave the door open. Under 70 words.
```

### 10.10 Twelve-month plan
```
Act as a solo-founder planner. Build a 12-month plan for {{PRODUCT}} after launch: one line per month (content, update, sale, outreach), two measurable goals per quarter, and the rule for when to stop investing in it.
```
