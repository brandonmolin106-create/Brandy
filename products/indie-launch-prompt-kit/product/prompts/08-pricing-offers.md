# 08 · Pricing and offers (8 prompts)

### 8.1 Price ladder
```
Act as a pricing analyst for digital products. Using templates/pricing-worksheet.md and these comparables: [paste 5 with prices], propose for {{PRODUCT}}: a free tier (what's in, what's out), a launch price, a full price, and a bundle price with another product of mine. Show the net per sale after platform fees for {{PLATFORMS}}. Justify each number in one sentence.
```

### 8.2 Launch discount design
```
Act as a promotions planner. Design the launch offer for {{PRODUCT}}: discount %, duration or buyer cap, the code name, where it appears (page, posts, emails), and the exact copy for the page badge, the X post and the email line. Explain why this beats both "no discount" and "50% off".
```

### 8.3 Pay-what-you-want vs fixed
```
Act as a marketplace analyst. For {{PRODUCT}} on {{PLATFORMS}}, compare pay-what-you-want with a minimum, fixed price, and fixed price with a free demo. For each: expected effect on volume, average price, reviews and support load. Recommend one and the minimum/anchor numbers.
```

### 8.4 Bundle logic
```
Act as a merchandiser. I have these products: [paste names and prices]. Propose 2 bundles with {{PRODUCT}}: contents, bundle price (60–75% of combined), the one-line pitch, and which to show on the store page of each product.
```

### 8.5 Price increase plan
```
Act as a pricing analyst. Write the plan for raising {{PRODUCT}} from launch price to full price: trigger (sales count and ratings), the date to announce, the announcement post under 200 chars, and how to honour buyers who purchased in the last 48 hours.
```

### 8.6 Regional pricing
```
Act as a global pricing specialist. For {{PRODUCT}} at {{PRICE}}, suggest prices for 10 countries using purchasing-power adjustment, and the copy for a regional discount code line on Gumroad or itch. Note which platforms do this automatically.
```

### 8.7 Objection: "too expensive"
```
Act as a sales coach. Write 5 ways to answer "too expensive" for {{PRODUCT}} without discounting: reframe to time saved, compare to the usual alternative, point to the free tier, show the guarantee, or agree and say who it is for. Each under 50 words, voice {{VOICE}}.
```

### 8.8 Break-even and goal
```
Act as a finance-minded founder. From these inputs: hours spent [n], hourly value [n], fixed costs [n], net per sale [n], write the break-even sales number, a realistic 30-day goal with three scenarios (quiet, normal, a post lands) and the one action that most changes the outcome.
```
