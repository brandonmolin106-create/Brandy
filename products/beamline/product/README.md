# Beamline — full edition

Bend a laser through mirrors, light every crystal, hit the target. 40 levels plus Zen mode.

## Play
Open `index.html` in any browser. Phone or desktop. Works offline after the first open.
On a phone: open it in Safari or Chrome, then Share → **Add to Home Screen** for a full-screen app.

## How to play
- Tap an empty cell to place a `/` mirror. Tap again to flip it to `\`. Tap a third time to remove it.
- You get a fixed number of mirrors per level (the par). Light every crystal and reach the target.
- Stars: 3 for finishing at par, 2 for one over, 1 for anything else. Progress saves in the browser.
- Levels unlock in order. Zen mode generates endless puzzles at level 25–40 difficulty.
- Undo, Reset and Sound buttons are under the board.
