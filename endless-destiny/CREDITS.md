# Credits

**Endless Destiny** is an Echoes in the Dark production.

- Song *Endless Destiny*: written and sung by **Sonny Molina**
- Studio: **Echoes in the Dark** (Brandon Molina)
- The leaf emblem: Echoes in the Dark. The in-game version is a vector redraw of the studio logo.

## Third-party assets

**CC0** (public domain). They are free for commercial use and need no attribution; they're listed
here anyway, with thanks.

| Boulders | `namaqualand_boulder_02`, `namaqualand_boulder_04` |
| Gorge cliffs | `namaqualand_cliff_02` |
