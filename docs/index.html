Meet the Team

Two people. One studio.

S

Sonny Molina

Frontman
The Voice Behind the Echoes

Sonny is the frontman, the face, The Voice Behind the Echoes and the emotional core of Echoes in the Dark. Sonny Molina writes by hand, one word at a time, and every word is written with the willingness to lose the line he was proudest of, until the feeling behind it becomes stronger than the words themselves.

Because no lyric is more important than the meaning behind it, he does not write simply to make a line sound beautiful, he writes to give a feeling somewhere to live. Every word has to carry something, a memory, a question, a struggle, a hope, or a piece of truth that might be difficult to say any other way.

And that is exactly what makes Sonny more than just the singer singing the creation.

He is the creation.

Not because every part of Echoes in the Dark is literally Sonny, but because the words carry the hand that chose them. The voice carries the life that shaped its meaning. And somewhere underneath all of it is Sonny.

B

Brandon Molina

Production, Brand and Releases
The Shop and the Website

Brandon runs the production, brand, releases, the shop, the website. His job in one line: take what Sonny writes and make sure the world feels it the way it was meant to.
