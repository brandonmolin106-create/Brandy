- "itch.io trending sound packs": sound packs run constant sales; 8-bit and UI packs both present; sellers advertise
  "no AI" as a feature. Our packs are synthesised by our own code, which we should say out loud.
- Decision: UI Sound Kit at A$12 with the generator included (tool angle). Buyer is app/web devs and video makers,
  a larger audience than game devs.
